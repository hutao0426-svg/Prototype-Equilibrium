Equilibrium

Web AR Application for Mental Health Awareness

What is Mental Health?

Mental health encompasses our emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It shapes how we think, feel, and act in our daily lives, influencing how we manage stress, build relationships, and make decisions across every stage of life.

Why It Matters

Prioritizing mental health fosters resilience, empathy, and personal balance. Understanding emotional challenges reduces social stigma, encouraging individuals to seek support early and create safer environments for growth and recovery.

Alignment with Sustainable Development Goals

Equilibrium directly supports the United Nations SDGs for global well-being and education.

SDG 3

Good Health & Well-being

Promoting mental health wellness and reducing social stigma by offering interactive Web AR visualization of emotional conditions.

SDG 4

Quality Education

Providing innovative, accessible, technology-driven educational resources to increase public mental health literacy.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

Ensuring open web access to mental health awareness tools regardless of geographical location or economic background.
